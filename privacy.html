Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Flow Traders Collective collects, why, and what rights you have. It applies to this website and to membership of our community.

Who we are

Flow Traders Collective is operated by Toskov Capital EOOD, company no. (EIK) 208361702, 19 Velcho Atanasov, Sofia, Bulgaria. We are the controller of the personal data described here. You can contact us about anything in this policy at toskovcapital@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Contact details. If you email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you write in your message.
  • Membership details. Membership is sold through LaunchPass and the community runs on Discord. When you join, we receive your email address, your Discord username and the status of your subscription. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not receive or store your full card details.
  • Community content. Messages, charts and files you post in the Discord server are visible to other members and to us.
  • Call recordings. Live calls are held on Zoom and are recorded so members can watch them later. If you speak, share your screen or appear on camera during a call, that will be part of the recording.
  • Website usage. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are viewed. It does not use cookies and reports aggregated figures that do not identify individual visitors.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use Cookiebot to ask for your consent to cookies. Cookies that are not strictly necessary, including those used to measure how our Google Ads campaigns perform, are only set if you agree. With your consent, Google may set cookies in your browser and collect information such as your IP address, device details and the pages you view, so that visits to this site can be linked to the ad that led to them.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. The list below is kept up to date by Cookiebot and shows the cookies this site uses.

Separately, the site stores one item in your browser's local storage to remember whether you chose the light or dark theme. The fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and Google receives your IP address.

Why we use your data

  • To provide your membership, including access to the community, calls and recordings. The legal basis is our contract with you.
  • To answer your questions and deal with requests. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • To understand how the website is used and keep it working. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running and improving the site.
  • To measure how our advertising performs. The legal basis is your consent, which you give or refuse through the cookie banner.
  • To keep accounting and tax records. The legal basis is our legal obligations under Bulgarian law.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the service providers we need to run the community and this website: LaunchPass (sign-up and subscription management), Stripe (payment processing), Discord (community), Zoom (live calls and recordings), Vercel (website hosting and analytics), Cookiebot (cookie consent) and Google (advertising measurement, fonts and email). We may also share data with our accountant, or with authorities where the law requires it.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, it is protected by an adequacy decision of the European Commission or by standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep membership details for as long as you are a member, and afterwards only for as long as accounting and tax law requires. We keep emails for as long as needed to deal with your request. Call recordings stay available to members while they remain useful as learning material; you can ask us to remove a part in which you appear.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to our use of it, and to receive it in a portable format. To use any of these rights, email toskovcapital@gmail.com.

You also have the right to complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (www.cpdp.bg), or to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

Age

Membership is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will publish the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
